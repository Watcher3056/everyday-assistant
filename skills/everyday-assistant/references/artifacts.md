# Visual Deliverables

For things the user will look at or use: an HTML comparison or tracker page, a website, a UI concept or style variants, a generated image or thumbnail, a catalog or presentation, a proof video or screenshot set. core.md and user-output.md still apply; this file adds only what is specific to building these.

## Before building

- **Requirements list.** Put every requirement from the user's messages (filters, sort, columns, radius, theme, size) into TASK.md in their words, including ones added later. Each gets ✅ or ❌ in the report.
- **One message per visual.** Know the one sentence the viewer should get in 5 seconds ("Take the Bosch: quietest and cheapest to run"; "Now the enemy stops at the ledge"). Two sentences make two visuals.
- **Show before building.** For a new screen, layout or style, show a picture first (mockup, screenshot or generated image) and build after the pick. For anything that goes to other people, show the final render before sending.
- **Use the real thing.** Real product photos, the project's real screenshots and art. Never invent a picture from a name alone.

## Look and taste

Defaults when the user or the project gives no direction. A project's own design wins.

| ✅ Want | ❌ Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Minimal but rich and "juicy": chunky shapes, flat emoji or icons with bold outlines, soft non-vibrant colour, tactile feedback (press, pop, bounce) | Generic glow, dark neon "engineering" panels, blueprint grids, gray MVP boxes, cold gray ("metal, dead"), warm cream ("too cozy") |
| Brotato, Cult of the Lamb, Gambonanza; story-like explainers (Red Blob Games, Bartosz Ciechanowski) | Brawl Stars-style mobile UI, "make it like Apple", Overwatch or Valorant |
| Real objects doing the thing (a hero hits three slimes) | Sliders, graphs, Gantt charts, five small panels in one canvas |
| One story top to bottom, demos where they're needed | Story and demo in two columns |

- **Every word on screen earns its place:** control labels, live values, names of things the viewer can't recognise, at most one hint until first use. No status lines ("System Online", a blinking dot), badges that repeat the title, or spoilers ("damage will deflect").
- **Polish is in the details:** the subject centred, no label overlapping it, effects drawn above the UI, a short build-up before a reveal. "Looks MVP" usually means spacing, type size, gray placeholders and too many small labels, not missing effects.

## Variants

- 2-4 variants of the same screen, side by side at the same size. Each is a different idea, not one layout recoloured.
- Use the real content (real photos, real text), and keep the rejected version next to them for reference.
- Name each, mark one Recommended with a one-line why, and ask with the choice menu. Variants shown without a pick and a question tend to get no answer at all.

## Pages: comparison, tracker, catalog

- **The first screen is the answer.** The pick with its photo, price and the 2-3 facts that decide it, then "if X matters more: Y". The method and sources go at the bottom, collapsed.
- **Compact cards.** Photo, name as a link, price, rating with review count, 3-4 facts as chips, one ✅ line and one ⚠️ line, "More" for the rest. Two rows of cards fit on one laptop screen.
- **Cards differ at a glance.** One verdict per card: ✅ Take, 🔁 Backup, ⏳ After the seller answers, ❌ Skip, each with its reason.
- **Trackers** (seller chats, orders, bookings): each card keeps a dated log of the chat, the deal status, the next step and whose it is, and a ready-to-send reply with its translation. Update it on every check and say in chat what changed.
- **Always reachable.** A self-contained file or a published page, opened for the user after each change. If a local server is needed, keep it running; never stop one the user may be looking at.
- **"Seen working" for a page** means opened at laptop and phone width, with every requested filter and button clicked.

## Generated images and thumbnails

- **Readable without the title.** Shrink it to its display size, show only the picture to a fresh sub-agent and ask "what is this about?". Redo until the answer matches the message.
- **3-5 things in frame.** In a comparison, the same subject on both sides, and no winner the content doesn't name. No text you didn't write.
- **3-4 takes.** Show the best one with the others next to it. A series shares its common parts (e.g. the "VS" badge); don't copy one image's style into the rest unless asked.
- If the project has its own visual-style skill or guide, follow it.

## Proof videos and screenshots

- **Real footage** from the product, not drawings or charts, unless asked.
- **A focused test scene per claim,** not a full fight where everything happens at once.
- **Was and now match:** same scenario, camera and length.
- **In the frame:** the claim as a title ("NOW: the enemy stops at the ledge"), a label on each actor, the counter that proves it, a legend. The camera shows every actor.
- **One video per claim,** named after the claim.
- **Watch the key moments before sending.** If the bug or the difference isn't visible, it isn't proof: record again.
